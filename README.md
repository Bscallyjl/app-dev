> # **Main Characters**
> Walter White / Heisenberg (Bryan Cranston). He is a high school chemistry teacher who turns to manufacturing and selling crystal methamphetamine after being diagnosed with terminal lung cancer.
>
> Jesse Pinkman (Aaron Paul). He is Walter's former student and his business partner in the meth trade.
>
> kyler White (Anna Gunn). She is Walter's wife, who becomes increasingly entangled in and conflicted by his criminal activities.
>
> Hank Schrader (Dean Norris). He is Walter's brother-in-law and a dedicated DEA agent tracking down the elusive "Heisenberg".
>
> Marie Schrader (Betsy Brandt). She is Skyler's sister and Hank's wife.
>
> Walter White Jr. / Flynn (RJ Mitte). He is Walter and Skyler's teenage son who has cerebral palsy.
>
> Saul Goodman / Jimmy McGill (Bob Odenkirk). He is the morally flexible criminal lawyer who represents Walt and Jesse.
>
> Gustavo "Gus" Fring (Giancarlo Esposito). He is a calm, ruthless drug kingpin who hides behind his fast-food franchise, Los Pollos Hermanos.
>
> Mike Ehrmantraut (Jonathan Banks). He is a former police officer who works as a private investigator, fixer, and enforcer for Gus Fring.
>
> Todd Alquist (Jesse Plemons). He is a cold, sociopathic associate who later works with Walt and the Aryan Brotherhood.
>
> Lydia Rodarte-Quayle (Laura Fraser). She is an anxious executive at Madrigal Electromotive who helps distribute meth overseas.



> Breaking Bad is an American neo-Western crime drama television series created by Vince Gilligan for AMC.
